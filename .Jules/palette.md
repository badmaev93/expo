## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility improvements for icon-only buttons\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons without `aria-label` attributes present a major accessibility barrier for screen reader users, as the purpose of the action is invisible without text.\n**Action:** Applied `aria-label`s to multiple icon-only action buttons across `TopNav.jsx`, `WidgetFrame.jsx`, and `Notifications.jsx` to ensure screen readers can announce the functionality correctly.
